Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Can I book the 7-day program directly?

No. There is one way in and it is the free 2-hour call. You book the call, you qualify inside it, and the private invite link to the 7-Day Program is sent to you afterwards. Nobody skips that step.

What actually happens in the free 2-hour call?

It's a live working session on your business, not a pitch. We run a competitor analysis with Claude on your market, show you where your time and money are leaking each month, map the AI era in plain language, and separate the handful of tools that are useful for your business from the ones you should ignore. The thread running through all of it is self-reliance — you leave able to do things yourself instead of waiting on an agency.

What does "qualify" mean? What if I don't?

Qualifying is not about your budget, your company size, or how technical you are. It's about readiness: whether you'll actually apply this, take responsibility for it, and stay with it when it gets uncomfortable. If you don't qualify, we'll tell you straight in the call, and you still keep everything the 2 hours gave you. No chasing, no hard feelings.

Is this really free?

Yes — both the 2-hour call and the 7-day program itself are free for this batch, with no card required. ENTALOP runs it because the best marketing we have is business owners who got real results.

I'm not technical at all. Will I keep up?

That's exactly who this is built for. No coding needed anywhere in the program. If you can use WhatsApp and a browser, you can do all of it. Every step is screen-shared live, and the recordings are yours to rewatch.

How much time do I need?

Two hours for the call, in one sitting — attend it properly or don't book it. If you're invited to the 7 days, that's roughly 60–90 minutes per session, held live in the evening so it works around business hours, with the recording out the same night.

Will this work for my type of business?

We work with e-commerce, real estate, healthcare, education, restaurants, and sales & service businesses every day. The four pillars — marketing, sales, operations and lead generation — exist in every business. The systems stay the same; we just point them at your model.

Do I need to pay for the AI tools?

No. Everything taught in the program can be done on free tiers. We'll also show you which paid upgrades are genuinely worth it later, and which ones aren't.

What about WhatsApp Business API charges?

The program itself is free. If you go on to run the WhatsApp Business API in production, Meta charges separately per conversation, and rates vary by message category and country. We cover exactly how that pricing works on Day 4, so there are no surprises.

My business is already online but growth has stalled. Is this still useful?

Very. A large share of attendees are in exactly that spot — website and social pages exist, but nothing is systemised. This program adds the automation layer that turns a static digital presence into a machine that generates and converts leads.

What happens right after I book?

You get an instant confirmation on WhatsApp and email with the link to your free 2-hour call and a short prep note, so you arrive with the right details about your business in hand.
